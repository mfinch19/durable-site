Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Durable - Longevity Exercises (“Durable”, “we”, “us”) is published by Matthew Finch. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. The short version: there are no accounts, your answers and workout history stay on your phone, and we use anonymous analytics to improve the app.

No accounts

Durable has no sign-up or sign-in, and we don’t ask for your email address. The app works on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your onboarding answers (the first name you enter, the body areas you want to work on, your goals, past injuries and age range) and your workout history and streak are stored only on your iPhone and are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Analytics

We use PostHog, an analytics service, to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. This information is tied to a random ID created on your device, not to an account or email address. It includes:

  • App events, such as screens viewed, buttons tapped and workouts completed.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and time zone.
  • Crash and error reports.
  • Session recordings: visual recordings of how screens are used in the app. Text you type and images are masked, but recordings can show other on-screen content, such as the options you select.

We use this only to understand and improve Durable. We don’t use it for advertising.

Exercise media

Exercise videos, images and audio are streamed from our media server (media.durable.fit). As with any download, standard request information such as your IP address reaches that server and its content delivery network.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple’s App Store and managed with RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and subscription status from Apple. Payments are handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads.
  • We don’t sell your data.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Service providers

We share information only with the providers that run parts of the app for us: PostHog (analytics), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Apple (purchases) and the hosting and delivery providers for our media server. They process it on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

To delete everything stored on your device, delete the app. To ask us to delete analytics data associated with your device, email matthewfinch100@gmail.com. Because we don’t know who you are, it helps if you tell us roughly when you used the app and what device you used.

Children

Durable is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we’ll delete any related analytics data.

Health information

Durable offers general fitness content and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new exercise routine, especially if you have an injury.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app or its release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email matthewfinch100@gmail.com.
