Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. They’re billed and managed by Apple:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  • Tap your name (Apple ID) at the top, then Subscriptions.
  • Choose Durable to change or cancel.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem page.

Restore purchases

If you’ve reinstalled Durable or moved to a new iPhone, open the paywall in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

On your phone. There are no accounts, and your answers, workout history and streak are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. See the Privacy Policy for details, including the anonymous analytics we use.

Can I change the body areas I’m working on?

Your daily routine is built from the body areas you chose during setup. There isn’t a setting to change them in the app yet. To choose new areas, you can delete and reinstall Durable and go through setup again, but note this also clears your workout history and streak (your subscription is kept; use Restore Purchase). If you’d rather not lose your history, email us.

Is this medical advice?

No. Durable offers general fitness content and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting, especially if you have an injury or a medical condition, and stop any exercise that causes pain.
